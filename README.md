# Decathlon Sales & Customer Analysis (Interactive Dashboard Creation using MS Excel)

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze Decathlon sales and customer data and create an interactive dashboard that helps understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and key business metrics.

The dashboard provides insights into revenue, sales trends, customer segments, product categories, and purchasing patterns to support data-driven business decisions.

## Dataset Used

Decathlon Synthetic Sales Dataset

* Data includes:

  * Customer Information
  * Product Information
  * Product Category
  * Sales
  * Quantity
  * Price
  * Revenue
  * Customer Demographics
  * Purchase Information

## Questions (KPIs)

1. What is the total revenue generated?
2. What is the total number of orders?
3. How many customers are included in the dataset?
4. What is the average order value?
5. Which product categories generate the highest sales?
6. Which products are the top performers?
7. Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
8. How does sales performance vary across different categories?
9. What are the major customer purchasing patterns?
10. How do sales change across different time periods?

## Process

1. Gathered business requirements and defined project objectives.
2. Imported the Decathlon sales dataset into Microsoft Excel.
3. Performed data cleaning and quality checks.
4. Removed duplicate records and handled missing values where required.
5. Standardized data formats and prepared the dataset for analysis.
6. Created calculated fields required for analysis.
7. Developed Pivot Tables for sales and customer analysis.
8. Created KPI cards for important business metrics.
9. Designed and formatted interactive dashboard visuals.
10. Created charts to analyze sales, customers, products, and categories.
11. Added filters and slicers for interactive analysis.
12. Generated business insights from the dashboard.
